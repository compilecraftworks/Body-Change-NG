Each skin has one self-contained folder:

BodySkin\<skin name>\
  Textures\              (copy the skin mod's textures folder here)
  profile.json            (required when the exact UV layout is ambiguous)

Without profile.json, Body Change NG automatically detects standard
femalebody/femalehands/femalefeet and malebody/malehands/malefeet texture
names plus their femalehead/malehead face textures. Argonian female/male and
Khajiit female/male folders are also detected separately. Player selection and
NPC distribution rules use the catalog separately and match race, sex, and
one exact body UV layout before applying a skin. Automatic folders should
include CBBE/3BA, UNP/BHUNP, UBE, HIMBO, SAM, or Vanilla in the pack name when
the texture namespace itself cannot distinguish the layout.

A pack may contain only some parts or channels. Only the supplied body, hands,
feet, or face channels are overridden; missing ones retain the actor's current
textures. Verified humanoid feet may share the body atlas; beast-race and
unknown layouts never do. UBE intentionally shares its Body atlas across body,
hands, and feet.

Use profile.json for an explicit custom or ambiguous selection. `Textures\...`
paths in that file are relative to this skin folder. Its optional `race` value
is `humanoid`, `argonian`, or `khajiit`. Set `uvLayout` to one of
`female-vanilla`, `cbbe`, `unp`, `ube`, `male-vanilla`, `himbo`, `sam`,
`argonian`, or `khajiit`.
